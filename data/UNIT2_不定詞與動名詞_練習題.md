## 一、文法選擇（打鐵趁熱 現學現賣，p.23）

1. (②) 選出正確的句子。
   ① To have breakfast every morning are important to us.
   **② To have breakfast every morning is important to us.**
   ③ To have breakfast every morning have important to us.
   ④ To have breakfast every morning has important to us.

2. (④) 選出正確的句子。
   ① To play basketball and to exercise in the gym is my hobbies.
   ② Playing basketball and to exercise in the gym is my hobbies.
   ③ To play basketball and to exercising in the gym is my hobbies.
   **④ To play basketball and to exercise in the gym are my hobbies.**

3. (①) 選出正確的句子。
   **① Father asks me not to play computer games.**
   ② Father asks I not to play computer games.
   ③ Father asks me not to playing computer games.
   ④ Father asks me don't to play computer games.

4. (③) 選出正確的句子。
   ① I need to a computer to doing my homework.
   ② I need to a computer to do my homework.
   **③ I need a computer to do my homework.**
   ④ I need a computer do my homework.

5. (②) 選出正確的句子。
   ① My father is too old to carrying those heavy boxes.
   **② My father is too old to carry those heavy boxes.**
   ③ My father is to old to carry those heavy boxes.
   ④ My father is to old too carry those heavy boxes.

6. (④) 選出正確的句子。
   ① Jenny practices to read and writing English every day.

### 四、依提示作答（p.27）

1. Jack drank a coke before lunch. + His mother told him not to do it.（合併成一句）
   → **Jack's mother told him not to drink a coke before lunch.**
2. We review lessons before tests. + It is important.（合併成一句）
   → **It is important to review lessons before tests.**
   > 手寫作答原文為 "review lesson"（單數），文法上應為複數 lessons，已在上方訂正。
3. It is interesting to play in the water.（用 V.-ing 當主詞改寫）
   → **Playing in the water is interesting.**
   > 手寫作答原文結尾誤寫為 "is important"，正確應為 "is interesting"（沿用原句意義），已在上方訂正。
4. Mother made me mop the floor.（用 want 改寫）
   → **Mother wants me to mop the floor.**
   > 手寫作答原文為 "Mother want me"，動詞應隨第三人稱單數主詞變化為 wants，已在上方訂正。

### 五、翻譯（p.28）

1. 他沒說再見就離開了。
   → **He left without saying goodbye.**
2. 這消息太好了，不會是真的。（消息 n. news；真的 adj. true）
   → **The news is too good to be true.**
   > 手寫作答原文結尾誤寫為 "ture"，正確拼字為 "true"，已在上方訂正。
3. 告訴他不要再來這裡了。
   → **Tell him not to come again.**
4. 早起有益健康。
   → **It is beneficial to health to get up early.**

## 錯誤重點回顧

- 動名詞/不定詞當主詞：**動詞需用單數**（Having good study habits **is**... ; It is important to review lessons ...）
- `enough money to buy`：`enough + N. + to V.`，不可用 `to V-ing`
- `want sb. to eat`：want 接受詞後需用不定詞 `to eat`，不可直接接 `V-ing`
- `be seen to go`：被動使役／感官動詞轉被動後，原形動詞要補回 `to`
- 動詞隨主詞人稱變化不可忘記（`Mother wants`，而非 `Mother want`）
