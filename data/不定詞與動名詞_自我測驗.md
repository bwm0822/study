# 不定詞與動名詞 自我測驗

> 依 [不定詞與動名詞_用法總整理.md](不定詞與動名詞_用法總整理.md) 的四大分類出題，共 20 題。答案與解析在文件最後。

## 一、選擇題

1. ( ) ______ every day is good to your health.
   ① Exercise ② Exercised ③ To exercise ④ Exercises

2. ( ) Going to school by bus is fast and convenient. = ______ is fast and convenient to go to school by bus.
   ① This ② It ③ That ④ There

3. ( ) My boyfriend likes ______ on the weekend.
   ① roller-skate ② roller-skating ③ roller-skated ④ to roller-skated

4. ( ) It started ______ suddenly during the picnic.
   ① rain ② rains ③ to rain ④ rained

5. ( ) I ______ to clean my room before my mom comes home.
   ① need ② needed to ③ needing ④ needs

6. ( ) They plan ______ to Japan next month.
   ① travel ② traveling ③ to travel ④ traveled

7. ( ) My hobby ______ stamps.
   ① is collect ② is collecting ③ is to collect ④ collects

8. ( ) My parents asked me ______ to church every Sunday.
   ① go ② going ③ to go ④ went

9. ( ) The strict teacher asked the students ______ English one hour a day.
   ① practice ② practicing ③ to practice ④ practiced

10. ( ) She has a lot of housework ______.
    ① do ② doing ③ to do ④ did

11. ( ) I need a pen ______ with.
    ① write ② writing ③ to write ④ wrote

12. ( ) He gave up ______ last year.
    ① smoke ② smoking ③ to smoke ④ smoked

13. ( ) Would you mind ______ the window for me?
    ① open ② opening ③ to open ④ opened

14. ( ) Jenny practices ______ and writing English every day.
    ① read ② reads ③ reading ④ to read

15. ( ) Leonardo is tired of ______ the same food every day.
    ① eat ② eating ③ to eat ④ ate

16. ( ) You can't take the dictionary without ______ me first.
    ① ask ② asking ③ to ask ④ asked

17. ( ) Remember ______ the door before you leave — it's not locked yet!
    ① lock ② locking ③ to lock ④ locked

18. ( ) I clearly remember ______ this movie with you last year.
    ① watch ② watching ③ to watch ④ watched

19. ( ) He stopped ______ a cigarette on his way to the meeting.
    ① smoke ② smoking ③ to smoke ④ smoked

20. ( ) She stopped ______ three years ago and has never touched a cigarette since.
    ① smoke ② smoking ③ to smoke ④ smoked

## 二、依提示改寫（判斷該用不定詞還是動名詞）

21. Playing basketball with Michael is interesting. （改用 It 為虛主詞開頭）
    → ___________________________________________

22. To play video games and to exercise in the gym are my hobbies. （改用動名詞當主詞）
    → ___________________________________________

23. He forgot to bring his umbrella this morning, so he got wet in the rain. （判斷：forget 用 to V 還是 V-ing？為什麼？）
    → ___________________________________________

24. I forgot meeting him at the party last week — someone had to remind me. （判斷：forget 用 to V 還是 V-ing？為什麼？）
    → ___________________________________________

---

## 解答與解析

1. **③ To exercise**（不定詞片語當句首主詞）
2. **② It**（虛主詞句型：It is + 形容詞 + to V）
3. **② roller-skating**（like 屬於分類一，to V／V-ing 皆可，此處考「互換」概念）
4. **③ to rain**（start 雖屬分類一，但此處只給不定詞選項，且 rain 需搭配 to + 原形動詞）
5. **① need**（need 為分類二動詞，主詞是 I，動詞用原形 need，後面才接 to V）
6. **③ to travel**（plan 只能接不定詞）
7. **③ is to collect**（不完全動詞 be 後面當主詞補語，只能用不定詞）
8. **③ to go**（ask + 受詞 + to V，受詞補語句型）
9. **③ to practice**（ask + 受詞 + to V，同上句型；注意不要跟「practice + V-ing」搞混，這裡的動詞是 ask，不是 practice）
10. **③ to do**（不定詞當形容詞，修飾前面的名詞 housework）
11. **③ to write**（不定詞當形容詞，修飾 pen；介詞 with 留在句尾）
12. **② smoking**（give up 只能接動名詞）
13. **② opening**（mind 只能接動名詞）
14. **③ reading**（practice 只能接動名詞，注意要和後面並列的 writing 詞性一致）
15. **② eating**（介係詞 of 後面只能接動名詞）
16. **② asking**（介係詞 without 後面只能接動名詞）
17. **③ to lock**（remember + to V：提醒「還沒做、要去做」的事）
18. **② watching**（remember + V-ing：回想「已經做過」的事）
19. **③ to smoke**（stop + to V：停下手邊動作，「去做」抽菸這件事）
20. **② smoking**（stop + V-ing：停止「抽菸」這個習慣，即戒菸）
21. **It is interesting to play basketball with Michael.**
22. **Playing video games and exercising in the gym are my hobbies.**（兩個動名詞並列，動詞用複數 are）
23. **用 to V（forgot to bring）**：因為是「該做卻忘記去做」——動作沒有發生。
24. **用 V-ing（forgot meeting）**：因為是「忘記自己做過的事」——動作已經發生過，只是不記得了。
